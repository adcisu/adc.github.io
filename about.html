ABOUT THE COLLECTIVE

A living archive of architecture.

The Architecture Documentation Collective is a student-run archive dedicated to documenting the lived experience of architectural education. We believe that the process, the sketches, the late nights, the site visits, the failed models and more is as important as the final product.

OUR MISSION

Architecture is a transformative experience. ADC exists to capture that transformation across all years.

By documenting field studies, building analyses, experimental projects, and personal reflections, we build a collective memory that future students can learn from and contribute to.
